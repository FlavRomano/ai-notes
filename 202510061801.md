By applying Tikhonov concept to gradient descent, we obtain a new learning rule
$$w_{new} = w + \eta \cdot \Delta w - 2 \lambda w$$ that is a **weight decay** technique
- basically add $2\lambda w$ to the gradient
- e.g with 0 gradient
	- it decreases the value of each $w$ 
	- with a fraction of the old $w$
