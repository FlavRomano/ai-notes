- measure the quality of approximation of $f$
- the distance between $h(x)$ and $d$
- given a pattern $x$, we use **inner loss function** $$L(h_{w}(x), d)$$
	- distance between $d$ and $h_{w}(x)$
	- we want to minimize it
- the error is an average from this inner loss function for a pattern
	- typically is the sum or a mean of the inner loss $L$ over the set of samples $$Loss(h_{w})=E(w)=\frac{1}{l}\sum_{p=1}^l L(h_{w}(x_p), d_{p})$$
		- in fact it's a mean e.g $$\frac{L(h_{w}(x_{1}, d_{1}) + L(h_{w}(x_{2}, d_{2}) )}{2}$$
	- loss function depends on the nature **of the task**
