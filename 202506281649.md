The product rule relates the **joint distribution** to the **conditional distribution** via $$p(x,y) = p(y\mid x) p(x)$$ The product rule is based upon the fact that 
- every joint distribution of two random variables can be factorized of two other distribution
- the two factors are
	- the marginal distribution of the first random variable $p(x)$
	- the conditional distribution of the second random variable $p(y\mid x)$
