Considering the classification problem
- we can also use a linear model
	- that works with **categorical targets**
	- e.g 0/1 or -1/+1
- we use a hyperplane $$w^Tx$$ assuming negative or positive values
	- help us to classify $x$
	- decide if a point $x$ belong to positive or negative zone of hyperplane
- can be viewed 
	- as the **allocation** of input space
	- into decision regions 
		- e.g region 0 or 1
