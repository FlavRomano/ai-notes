Machine Learning algorithms follow one of two timing paradings:
1. **eager learning**
	- the algorithm analyze the training data
	- construct an explicit hypothesis (model) during the initial training phase
	- e.g linear models use the training phase to find parameters that define a global hypothesis
2. **lazy learning**
	- the algorithm store the training data
	- wait until test data point is presented 
		- to construct an *ad-hoc* hypothesis specifically for that single point

- lazy
