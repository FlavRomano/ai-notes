Given the TR set $$T=\{(x_{i}, d_{i})\}\quad\forall i = 1 \dots N$$
we want to find
- the optimal Lagrangian multipliers $$\alpha_{i},\alpha_{i}'\quad \forall i = 1\dots N$$
	- $\alpha_i$ for points **above** the tube (overestimation)
	-  $\alpha_i'$ for points **under** the tube (underestimation)
- which minimizes the objective function $$Q({\alpha}, {\alpha}')
= \sum_{i=1}^{N} d_i (\alpha_i - \alpha_i') - \varepsilon \sum_{i=1}^{N} (\alpha_i + \alpha_i') - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{(i,j)=1}^{N} (\alpha_i - \alpha_i')(\alpha_j - \alpha_j') k({x}_i, {x}_j)$$

Under the constraints
- Lagrangian multipliers between 0 and hyperparameter $C$ $$0\leq \alpha_{i}\leq C\quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
$$0\leq \alpha_{i}'\leq C\quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
- The overall force from the data points trying to pull the regression line up must equal the force from those pulling it down $$\sum_{i=1}^N (\alpha_{i} - \alpha_{i}') = 0$$so the **total upward push** ($\alpha_i$​) must **balance** the **total downward push** ($\alpha_i'$​)
