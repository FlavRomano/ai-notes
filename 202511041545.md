A support vector $x^{(s)}$ is 
- a vector on the edge separating hyperplane edge
- so formally satisfies the hyperplane (canonical) equation *exactly* $$d^{(s)}(w^T x^{(s)} + b) = 1$$

![[Pasted image 20251104154707.png]]

Support vectors are the *closest data points* to the hyperplane.
