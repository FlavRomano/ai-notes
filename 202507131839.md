The execution plan in Spark is a DAG.
- serves as the logical blueprint of a Spark application

---
Why a DAG?
- it's a graph
	- The nodes represents the data (RDD).
	- The edges represent the **transformations** (functions/operations) to be applied
- it's directed
	- the edges have a **one way flow**
	- representing the sequence of data transformations
- it's acyclic
