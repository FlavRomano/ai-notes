In the **voting schema** (or committee), we have several trained models $$h_1​(x),h_2​(x),…,h_k​(x)$$
Instead of trusting just one model
- we combine their outputs
- to form a **single prediction** $$o(x) = agg(h_{1}(x), h_{2}(x),\dots, h_{k}(x))$$ the $agg$ function is defined by the user.

In general $$Loss(E_{i}(h_{i})) \leq E_{i}(Loss(h_{i}))$$so 
- the square error of a committee 
- is $\leq$
- then the mean of square error
---

- For regression 
	- the natural aggregation is simply the mean of the individual models $$o(x) = \frac{1}{k} \sum_{i=1}^k h_{i}(x) = \overline{h}(x)$$
- For classification
	- **majority voting**
	- the ensemble predicts the class that most models agree on $$o(x) = \text{mode}(h_{1}(x), h_{2}(x), \dots, h_{k}(x))$$
