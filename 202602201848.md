Knowing an event $y$, the probability of event $x$ conditioned by $y$ is defined by $$p(x \mid y) \equiv \frac{p(x,y)}{p(y)}$$ By the property $$p(x,y) = p(y,x)$$ we arrive at bayes rule $$p(x\mid y) = \frac{p(y\mid x)p(x)}{p(y)}$$
