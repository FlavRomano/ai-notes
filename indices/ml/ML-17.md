2.  **Theoretical Foundations: Why Deep?**
    *   **Hierarchical Abstraction:** Compositionality of features (e.g., edges to shapes to objects).
    *   **Efficiency (No-flattening):** The exponential gain in representational power of deep circuits versus shallow ones (demonstrated via the **Parity Function/XOR** logic gate example).
	    * [[202602231550|no flattening]]
    *   **Inductive Bias:** The assumption that real-world data (images, language) is generated by a composition of factors.
	    * [[202602231607|deep learning inductive bias]]
    *   **Curse of Dimensionality:** How deep distributed representations allow non-local generalization.
	    * [[202602231615|how deep networks beat curse of dimensionality]]

3.  **Representation Learning**
    *   Moving from manual feature engineering to automatic feature discovery.
    *   **Distributed Representation vs. Symbolic:** Comparison of dense vectors vs. one-hot encoding.
	    * [[202602231641|distributed representation]]
    *   **Disentangling Concepts:** Sharing attributes to improve generalization.
    *   **Word Embeddings:** Learning semantic similarity automatically (NLP examples).

4.  **Building and Initializing Deep Networks**
    *   **Unsupervised Pre-training:** Greedy layer-wise training using Autoencoders (denoising, under/over-complete).
	    * [[202602231650|autoencoder]]
	    * [[202602231705|greedy layer-wise pre-training]]
    *   **Transfer Learning:**  Reusing representations from pre-trained models (e.g., AlexNet) for new tasks via fine-tuning.

5.  **Key Training Techniques**
    *   **Gradient Issues:** Addressing Vanishing and Exploding gradients.
	    * [[202602231801|vanishing gradient]]
	    * [[202602231814|exploding gradients]]
		* [[202602231818|gradient clipping]]
    *   **Activation Functions:** The shift from Sigmoids to **ReLU** (Rectified Linear Units) to prevent saturation and improve sparsity.
	    * [[202602231807|RELU]]
    *   **Batch Normalization:** Normalizing layer inputs to accelerate training and reduce internal covariate shift.
	    * [[202602231842|batch normalization]]

6.  **Regularization Methods**
    *   **Dropout:** Randomly disabling units during training to simulate model ensembling and prevent co-adaptation.
	    * [[202602231853|dropout]]
	    * [[202602231907|implicit bagging]]
