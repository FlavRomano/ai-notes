4.  **Self-Organizing Maps (SOM/Kohonen Maps)**
    *   **Concept:** Mapping high-dimensional input to a low-dimensional grid (Lattice).
	    * [[202602271020|SOM]]
    *   **Goal:** Topology-preserving mapping (preserving neighborhood relations).
    *   **Biological Inspiration:** Competitive learning and the Homunculus (Somatosensory cortex).

5.  **SOM Learning Algorithm**
	- [[202602271028|learning algorithm]]
    *   **Competitive Stage:** Finding the "Winner" unit (Euclidean distance).
	    - [[202602271101|competitive stage]]
    *   **Cooperative Stage:** Updating weights of the winner *and* its topological neighbors.
	    * [[202602271102|cooperative stage]]
    *   **Neighborhood Function:** The "Soft-max" adaptation rule (decreasing radius over time).
