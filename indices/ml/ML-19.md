4.  **The K-means Algorithm**
    *   **On-line version:** Stochastic Gradient Descent update rule.
    *   **Batch version (LBG):** Assignment and Centroid re-computation steps.
    *   **Pros/Cons:** Simplicity vs. Local minima and initialization dependency.
    *   **Limitations:** Lack of topological order and visualization capabilities.

5.  **Self-Organizing Maps (SOM/Kohonen Maps)**
    *   **Concept:** Mapping high-dimensional input to a low-dimensional grid (Lattice).
    *   **Goal:** Topology-preserving mapping (preserving neighborhood relations).
    *   **Biological Inspiration:** Competitive learning and the Homunculus (Somatosensory cortex).

6.  **SOM Learning Algorithm**
    *   **Competitive Stage:** Finding the "Winner" unit (Euclidean distance).
    *   **Cooperative Stage:** Updating weights of the winner *and* its topological neighbors.
    *   **Neighborhood Function:** The "Soft-max" adaptation rule (decreasing radius over time).

7.  **Visualization and Applications**
    *   Visualizing the emerging topological order (grid unfolding).
    *   **U-Matrix:** Visualizing cluster boundaries and densities.
    *   **Application Example:** Clustering World Welfare indicators.
