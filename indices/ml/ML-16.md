1.  **Bias-Variance Decomposition Theory**
    *   Definitions of the three error components: Bias (systematic error), Variance (sensitivity to data), and Noise (irreducible error).
	    * [[202511121657|introduction on bias-variance]]
    *   Mathematical derivation of the expected prediction error using Squared Error Loss ($L_2$).
	    *  [[202511121657|introduction on bias-variance]]
    *   The decomposition formula: $\text{Error} = \text{Bias}^2 + \text{Variance} + \text{Noise}$.
	    * [[202511131658|variance]]
	    * [[202511131704|decomposition]]

3.  **Regularization and the Trade-off**
    *   The impact of the regularization parameter ($\lambda$) on model complexity.
	    * [[202511131735|lambda on bias-variance]]
    *   **High $\lambda$:** High Bias, Low Variance (Underfitting/Rigid models).
    *   **Low $\lambda$:** Low Bias, High Variance (Overfitting/Complex models).

4.  **Ensemble Learning**
	- [[202511141726|introduction on ensemble learning]]
    *   **Voting/Averaging:** Combining multiple models to improve performance.
	    * [[202511141731|committee/majority voting]]
    *   **Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating):** Training on bootstrapped subsets to reduce **variance** (e.g., maximizing margins).
	    * [[202511141752|bagging]]
    *   **Boosting (e.g., AdaBoost):** Sequential training weighting difficult instances to reduce **bias** (improving weak learners).
	    * [[202511141759|boosting]]
