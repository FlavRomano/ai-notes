Given two random variables $X:\Omega \to \mathcal{T}_{X}$, $Y:\Omega \to \mathcal{T}_{Y}$
- if we consider marginal probability of $X$
	- only instances where $X=x$
- then the fraction of instances which $Y=y$ is called **conditional probability** $$p(y\mid x)$$
