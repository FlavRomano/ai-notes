A learner overfits the data when it outputs a hypothesis $h\in H$ that
- generalize bad 
	- generalization error (risk) $R$ is high
- and is accurate on training set
	- empirical (training) error $E$ is low
	- fits well the training data

---

That's why Statistical Learning Theory is a thing
