In the $VC$-bound $$R \le R_{emp} + \varepsilon \left( \frac{1}{l} , VC, \frac{1}{\delta} \right)$$ we call $\varepsilon(\cdot)$ $VC$-penalty 
- is the “price” we pay for choosing a richer hypothesis space
- so it's something that grows with VC-dimension

In general
- $\dfrac{1}{l}$ implies that
	- more data 
	- smaller $\varepsilon$
- larger VC implies bigger penalty
- $\dfrac{1}{\delta}$ is the confidence parameter 
	- so smaller $\delta$ 
	- higher probability guarantee
	- looser bound
