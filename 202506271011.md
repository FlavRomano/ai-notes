Q: What are the two major interpretations of probability in ML and statistics?

E:
- The Bayesian interpretation
	- uses probability to specify the **degree of uncertainty** that user has about an event
- The frequentist interpretations
	- considers the **relative frequencies of events of interest** to the total number of events that occurred

C: The two major interpretations are the bayesian interpretation, which uses probability to specify the uncertainty degree that user has about an event, and the frequentist interpretations that considers relative frequencies of events of interest to the total number of events that occurred.
