The median is the **middle** value if we sort the values.
- 50% are greater than the median
- 50% are smaller than the median

Speaking of values
- For distributions
	- which are asymmetric or have long tails
- the median provides an estimate of a typical value 
- that is closer to human intuition than the mean.
