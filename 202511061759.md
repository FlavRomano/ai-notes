Given the TR set $$T=\{(\Phi(x_{i}), d_{i})\}\quad\forall i = 1 \dots N$$
we want to find 
- the optimal Lagrangian multipliers $$\alpha_{i} \quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
- which maximize the objective function $$Q(\alpha) = \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i,j=1}^N \alpha_{i} \alpha_{j} d_{i} d_{j} k(x_{i},x_{j})$$ where $k(x_{i},x_{j})$ is the component in the $i$-th row and $j$-column of the Kernel matrix.

satisfying the constraints
- Lagrangian multipliers between 0 and hyperparameter $C$ $$0\leq \alpha_{i}\leq C\quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
- the weighted contributions of the Lagrange multipliers from both positive and negative samples are balanced $$\sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} =0$$
	- this keeps the hyperplane **centered between both classes** rather than being dragged toward one side.
	- optimization doesn't favor the positive or negative side of the margin
