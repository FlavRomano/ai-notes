Let $Z$ be a discrete random variable
- with possible values $$z_{i}\quad i=1..l$$
- with probability distribution $P(Z)$

The expected value, or mean, of $Z$ is $$\overline{Z} = E_{P}[Z] = \sum_{i=1}^l z_{i} \cdot P(z_{i})$$
