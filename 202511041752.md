We want a model that performs well 
- on TR examples
- on unseen examples

This is captured by the generalization bound 
$$R \le R_{emp} + \varepsilon \left( \frac{1}{l} , VC, \frac{1}{\delta} 
\right)$$

For linear classifiers in $\mathbb{R}^m$, Vapnik theorem showed that 

Given 
- the diameter $D$ of the smallest ball around training data $$x_i \forall i=1...N$$ 

the **VC dimension** can be bounded by $$VC \leq min\left( \left\lfloor  \frac{D^2}{\rho^2}  \right\rfloor ,m_0​ \right)+1$$where $m_{0}$ is the input space dimension
remark: it's an upper bound for the VC-dim.

So 
- a larger margin $\rho$ 
	- the classifier has fewer degrees of freedom 
- means a smaller upper bound on the VC dimension
	- it’s more stable and cannot overfit tiny variations in the data
- generalize well to unseen data
