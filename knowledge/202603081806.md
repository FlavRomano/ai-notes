> Bayesian Networks are used to model asymmetric dependencies, so they are not good to model **symmetric dependencies**. Variables that influence each other can't be modeled.

**Markov Random Fields** are **undirected graphical models** designed specifically to handle symmetric dependencies that directed models cannot represent.

![[Drawing 2026-03-08 18.11.52.excalidraw]]

Generally if a Bayesian Network can't be d-separated, then needs an undirected graph.
