Hidden Markov model are meant to model complex relationships between observations that simple Markov chains can't.

![[Pasted image 20260612123527.png]]

HMM are a stochastic process that separates in two layers
- transition dynamics $S_t$
	- hidden states
	- they follow a Markov chain
- observations $Y_{t}$
	- observed data
	- generated from a given state $S_{t}$
