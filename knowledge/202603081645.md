The markov blanket $Mb(Y)$ of a node $Y$ 
- is the minimal set of vertices that **shield the node** from the rest of the bayesian network
- the behavior of a node ca be **completely determined and predicted** from the knowledge of its Markov Blanket $$P(Y\mid Mb(Y), Z) = P(Y\mid Mb(Y))\; \forall Z\notin Mb(Y)$$

![[Drawing 2026-03-08 16.54.48.excalidraw]]

$$Mb(Y) = Pa(Y) \cup Ch(Y) \cup Pa(Ch(Y))$$

> Is the smallest set of observation that I need to perform inference on Y

If $Z \notin Mb(Y)$, we can conclude that $$Z  \perp\!\!\!\perp_{\mathcal{G}}  Y \mid Mb(Y) \implies Z \perp\!\!\!\perp  Y \mid Mb(Y)$$ by global markov property
