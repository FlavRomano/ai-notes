Bayesian inference has a tractability issue, because requires considering **all possible parameter values** (or hypotheses).

Given a model parameterized by $\theta$, after observing data $d$
- bayesian inference predicts a new observation $X$  by averaging over every possible $\theta$ $$P(X\mid d) = \int P(X\mid \theta) P(\theta \mid d) \; d\theta$$
- the integral is impossible to compute exactly because
	1. **high dimensional parameter spaces**
	2. **complicated posterior distribution**
		- $P(\theta\mid d)$ may not have a simple analytical form
	3. **no closed form solution**
		- integral cannot be solved symbolically
