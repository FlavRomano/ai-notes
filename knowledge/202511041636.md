To solve the SVM quadratic problem is used the **Lagrangian multipliers method**.

- Construct the Lagrangian function corresponding to the [[202511041637|quadratic optimization problem]]$$\begin{align}
J(w,b,\alpha) &= \psi(w) -  \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i}(d_{i}(w^T x_{i} + b) - 1) \\
&= \frac{1}{2} w^T w - \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i}(d_{i}(w^T x_{i} + b) - 1)
\end{align}$$
- $\alpha_{i} \geq 0\; \forall i = 1\dots N$ are **Lagrangian multipliers**
- each term in the sum 
	- **corresponds to a constraint** of the primal problem
- $J$ must be 
	- **minimized** w.r.t $w$ and $b$ $$\implies \frac{\partial}{\partial w}J =0,\quad \frac{\partial}{\partial b} J = 0$$ are KKT-conditions.
	- **maximized** w.r.t $\alpha$ 
- The solution corresponds to a **saddle point** of $J$
	- in which every constraint is satisfied $$\alpha_{i}(d_{i}(w^Tx_{i} + b) - 1) = 0\quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$

To conclude, minimize $J$ w.r.t $w$ implies $$w = \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} x_{i}$$ and also w.r.t $b$ $$\sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} = 0$$

---

The optimal values of $w$ is $$w_{o} = \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{o,i} d_{i}x_{i}$$ so we obtain in the [[202511041521|optimal hyperplane]] expressed as $$\begin{align}
w_{o}^Tx+ b_{o} &= 0 \\
\iff \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{o,i} d_{i}x_{i}^T + b_{o} &= 0
\end{align}$$
To conclude, minimize $J$ w.r.t $w$ implies $$w = \sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} x_{i}$$ and also w.r.t $b$ $$\sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} = 0$$

--- 
To obtain the Lagrangian multipliers $\{\alpha_{i}\}\quad \forall i=1\dots N$ we solve the problem in [[202511041714|dual form]].

$\alpha_i$ controls 
- how much influence each training point $x_{i}$ has on the hyperplane
