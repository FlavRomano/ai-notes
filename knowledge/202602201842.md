Given a joint distribution $p(x,y)$ the distribution of a single variable is given by $$p(x) = \sum_{y} p(x,y)$$
- $p(x)$ is named **marginal** of the **joint probability distribution** $p(x,y)$
- the process of computing the marginal from a joint distribution is called **marginalisation** 

$$p(x_{1}, \dots, x_{i-1}, x_{i+1}, \dots, x_{n}) = \sum_{x_{i}} p(x_{1}, \dots, x_{n})$$
