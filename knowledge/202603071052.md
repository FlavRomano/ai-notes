The expectation is a linear operator, therefore it respects
- **constants** $$\mathbb{E}_{x}[k] = k$$
- **scaling** $$\mathbb{E}_{x}[k \cdot f(x)] = k \cdot \mathbb{E}[f(x)]$$
- **additivity** $$\mathbb{E}_{x}[f(x) + g(x)] = \mathbb{E}_{x}[f(x)] + \mathbb{E}_{x}[g(x)] $$
- **independence** $$\mathbb{E}_{x}[f(x) \cdot g(y)] = \mathbb{E}_{x}[f(x)] \cdot \mathbb{E}_{x}[g(y)] $$
