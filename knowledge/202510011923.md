When the weight vector $w$ is multiplied by a scalar $K$, 
- the new decision boundary is defined by the equation $$K(\mathbf{w}^T\mathbf{x} + w_0) = 0$$
- Since the set of points $x$ that satisfy this new equation 
	- is exactly the same set that satisfies the original equation 
	- **the geometric position of the decision boundary remains the same**
