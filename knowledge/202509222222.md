In Machine Learning is preferred Search Bias over Language Bias for two reasons
1. Language bias places **constraints in the model**, in the hypothesis space $H$
	- limiting the set of hypotheses expressible
	- if target concept **is not expressible** in this restricted $H$
		- it cannot be represented
		- so it's excluded *a priori*
2. Search bias involves constraints or preferences in the learning algorithm/search strategy
	- we are not limiting the hypothesis space $H$
	- we perform an **incomplete search strategy**
		- it helps generalization
		- the search can't be exhaustive
			- $H$ is too large
