In classical ML, Bagging improves performance by 
- training 
	- many different models
	- on different subsets of data
- averaging their prediction

This reduce effectively the variance (low variance is good!), reducing overfitting.

In Dropout the bagging is implicit (simulated)
- creates $2^n$ different possible sub-networks
	- the *"different models"*
- that shares the same weights
	- many paths sharing the same link
