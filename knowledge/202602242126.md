A randomized neural network has two parts

![[Pasted image 20260224212930.png]]

A classic MLP try to learn 
- the feature representation (hidden layers)
- the decision (output layer)

Instead of learning feature representation, randomized neural network rely on the probability that
- a random projection into a much higher-dimensional space 
- will make data easier to separate linearly
