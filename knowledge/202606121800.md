Given
- a trained model
- an observed sequence $$\mathbf{y} = (y_{1}, \dots, y_{t})$$

we want to find $$s^* = (s_{1}^* , \dots, s_{T}^*) = \arg \max_{s} P(Y, S=s)$$

> what is the single most likely hidden states sequence?

We are maximizing simultaneously 
- transitions probability
- emissions probability
