Batch normalization is the technique used in deep neural network training
- to ensure normalization in input of each layer

In fact, for each mini-batch $mb$
1. It calculates $mb$ mean and variance
2. Normalize $mb$ by subtracting mean and dividing by $mb$ standard deviation

There are many pros:
- optimization landscape become smoother
	- gradient flow stabilized
	- guarantee high learning rate without explosion
	- faster training
- robust network
	- less sensitivity to initial starting weight
- regularization
	- per batch normalization uses noisy mean and variance
		- adds noise to training
	- acts as regularizer to prevent overfitting
