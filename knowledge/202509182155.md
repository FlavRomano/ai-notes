- $M$ is a very big matrix
	- represents all the amazon customers
- $A$ are the merchandise
	- tall and thin
	- $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times k}$
	- column of $M$
- $B$ is the number of items purchased for the customer
	- fat and large
	- $B \in \mathbb{R}^{k \times m}$
	- column of $B$

We want to compress $M$ with a product of two matrix $A$ and $B$.
- we need to find $k$ 
	- a lot smaller then $n$ and $m$
- we want to find features such that I can represent well $M$ through $A$ and $B$ $$min_{A,B} \mathcal{L}(A,B) = \lvert \lvert M- AB \rvert  \rvert $$
- $AB$ is what my model predict
	- I want to be it as close as possible to the starting matrix $M$
	- the optimal solution of this problem have to do with eigenvectors

---

One of the application of this model is image compression.
