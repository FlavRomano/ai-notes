Two nodes $Y_{i}$ and $Y_{j}$ in a bayesian network $\mathcal{G}$ are **d-separated by $Z \subset \mathcal{V}$** 

$$Y_{1} \perp\!\!\!\perp_{\mathcal{G}} Y_{2} \mid Z$$

> if and only if all undirected paths between $Y_i$ and $Y_{j}$ are d-separated by $Z$
