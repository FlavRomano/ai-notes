Deep networks exploit the fact that complex functions 
- are often compositions of simpler functions
	- feature learned at one level
	- are reused by many features in deeper layers

In flattened network there are **no feature reuse**
- concept are learned each time they appears in different context
	- aka combinatorial representation of features
	- e.g red car without concept of redness is a completely different sample car
