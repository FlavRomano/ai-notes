After the hidden state is determined, is used to generate the observation.

Each hidden state $S_{i}$ holds an emission distribution $b_{i}(y_{t})$ $$b_{i}(y_{t}) = P(Y_{t} = y_{t} \mid S_{t} = i)$$ probability of observing $y_t$ at state $S_i$
