Ensemble learnings is 
- a machine learning strategy 
- that **combines multiple models** 
	- _base learners_ 
- to produce a single, often stronger, predictive model

Instead of relying on one hypothesis $h(x)$ 
- we train several models $$h_1(x),h_2(x),…,h_{k}(x)$$each trained perhaps slightly differently, and then **aggregate** their predictions.

---

The simplest case is the [[202511141731|voting schema]], it produces
- an averaging of the predictions
- that reduce the overall **variance**

Diversity makes 
- the errors uncorrelated
- the average becomes **more reliable**

> wisdom of the crowd

---

We got two types of ensemble strategy
- [[202511141752|bagging]]
	- e.g random forests
- [[202511141759|boosting]]
	- e.g AdaBoost
