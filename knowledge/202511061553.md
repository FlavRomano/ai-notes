Considering an different version of SVM from [[202511041520|hard margin]].

In soft margin SVM we admit misclassification, patterns that violates the margin.

![[Pasted image 20251106155509.png]]

At least one point violate the condition $$d_{i} (w^T x_{i} + b) \geq 1$$ we can get a *benefit*, by exploiting this constraint relaxation we can have a larger margin. And so a better model. 

Formally we admit point inside the margin through non-negative scalar, named [[202511061600|slack variable]].

By admitting such points inside the margin, the [[202511041752|Vapnik's Theorem]] does not hold anymore.

The [[202511061608|primal problem]] and the [[202511061620|dual problem]] for soft margin are indeed different from the hard margin one. 

Because slack variables and new constraints, also [[202511061636|KKT conditions]] are updated. In particular bounds the multipliers instead of the $\alpha_{i} > 0$ of the hard margin SVM.

As the hard margin, even in this case we solve the [[202511061620|dual problem]].
