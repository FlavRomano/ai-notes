*   **Global Markov Condition:** Requires the graph to represent *only* true conditional independencies. It ensures the graph doesn't lie by showing a d-separation where an independence doesn't actually exist. (Graph $\implies$ Distribution).
*   **Faithfulness Condition:** Requires the graph to represent *all* conditional independencies. It ensures the graph doesn't miss any hidden independencies that exist in the data. (Distribution $\implies$ Graph).
