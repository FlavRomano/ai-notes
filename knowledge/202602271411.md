> Once the network is unfolded, we can calculate the loss at the output and work backward to update the weights

The Back-Propagation Through Time is the standard algorithm (used in PyTorch, etc.), it works in epochs.

1. Forward pass
	- we need the entire sequence into the network
		- from $t=0$ to $t=T$
	- we calculate the states and outputs for every step and store them
2. Calculate error
	- we measure the error at every time step
	- can be just at the end (task dependent choice)
3. Backward pass
	- we calculate the gradient of the error wrt weights $W$
	- moving from $t=T$ back to $t=0$
