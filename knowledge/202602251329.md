A data manifold is the shape or structure that some data forms inside a larger space.

Consider a space that models a picture 100x100 pixel of a man (so $\mathbb{R}^{10,000}$). Most combinations of pixels look like static noise, but only a tiny slice of that space looks like a human face. That specific shape where the faces exists is the **manifold**
