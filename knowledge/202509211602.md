- The model has a teacher
	- training examples are **labeled** $$<input, output> = <x,d>$$
		- $d$ is the target value, or label
			- could be categorical or numerical
			- classification vs regression
		- is given by the teacher according to $f(x)$
	- for each input, the correct output is already provided
- The model **doesn't know $f$**
	- by learning from its examples
	- wants to build a good approximation of $f$
		- hypothesis $h$
		- that generalize well on unseen data $x'$
