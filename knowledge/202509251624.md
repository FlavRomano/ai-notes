- with a lot of data
	- big $l$ near infinite
	- the $R_{emp}$ begin similar to $R$ 
- with simple model
	- low $VC-dim$ 
	- high $R_{emp}$
	- model is not flexible
	- doesn't fit the data
		- we fall in underfitting
- higher VC-dim
	- fix $l$
	- lower $R_{emp}$
	- VC-conf increase
		- because VC-dim increase
		- big epsfun
	- model overfits

Model flexibility is a tradeoff
- stay in the middle point
	- Structural risk minimization
	- minimize the bound
		- the sum of $R_{emp}$ and epsfun
