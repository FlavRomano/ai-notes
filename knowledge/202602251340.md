In a Voronoi Diagram 
- each cell
	- consisting of all points closer to centroids
	- than to any other patterns
- the segments
	- are all the points in the plane
	- that are equidistant to two patterns

Finding the perfect optimal reference vector is NP-complete. Mostly we want good enough solution that minimizes loss.
