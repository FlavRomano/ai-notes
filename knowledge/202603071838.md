Visually, this structure consists of two parent nodes ($Y_1$ and $Y_3$) that both point **into** a single child node ($Y_2$).

The joint probability for this structure factorizes is $$P(Y_1, Y_2, Y_3) = P(Y_1) P(Y_3) P(Y_2 \mid Y_1, Y_3)$$

This structure behaves uniquely compared to the confounder and chain. **Its independence logic is reversed.**

> Observation **unlocks** the path (creates dependence).

*   $Y_2$ (the Common Effect) is Unobserved ![[Drawing 2026-03-07 18.43.56.excalidraw]]
    *   **Result:** $Y_1$ and $Y_3$ are **marginally independent** $$Y_1 \perp Y_3$$
    * The path is naturally "blocked."
    * Since $Y_1$ and $Y_3$ are separate causes, knowing one tells you nothing about the other if you don't know the outcome ($Y_2$). 

*   $Y_2$ is Observed  ![[Drawing 2026-03-07 18.43.22.excalidraw]]
    *   **Result:** $Y_1$ and $Y_3$ become **conditionally dependent** $$Y_1 \not\perp Y_3 \mid Y_2$$
    *   Observing the node **"unlocks the path."** 
    *   this effect occurs even if we observe a **descendant** of $Y_2$, not just $Y_2$ itself.
