The Binomial describes the distribution of a discrete two-state variable $x$ with $dom(x) = \{ 0,1 \}$. 

The probability that in $n$ Bernoulli Trials will be $k$ success states $1$ $$x^1, \dots,x^n \quad p(y=k\mid \theta) = {n \choose k} \theta^k (1- \theta)^{n-k}, \quad y=\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{I}[x^i] = 1$$

The mean and variance are $$mean(y) = n\theta\quad var(y)=n\theta(1-\theta)$$
