Given a bilinear mapping $\Omega$ 
- has 2 arguments
- it's linear in each argument

looking at a vector space $V$, it holds that $\forall x,y,z\in V, \lambda,\psi\in \mathbb{R}$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Omega(\lambda x + \psi y, z) &= \lambda \Omega(x,z) + \psi \Omega (y,z)\\
\Omega(x,\lambda y + \psi z) &= \lambda \Omega(x,y) + \psi \Omega (x,z)
\end{aligned}
$$
