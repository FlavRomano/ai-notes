Given two random variables $X$ and $Y$, we define the joint distribution as the probability of the intersection of both events $$P(X=x_{i},Y=y_{j}) = P\left( X=x_{i} \cap Y=y_{j} \right)$$ 

![[Pasted image 20260306174414.png]] 

$n_{ij}$ is the number of events with state $x_{i}$ and $y_{j}$.

- every joint distribution of two random variables can be written as product of two other distributions thanks to product rule.
