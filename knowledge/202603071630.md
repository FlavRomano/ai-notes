Instead of computing the entire joint probability table row by row, we can exploit the chain rule to decompose the joint.

$$\begin{align}
P(Y_{1}, Y_{2}, Y_{3}) &= P(Y_{1}) P(Y_{2} \mid Y_{1}) P(Y_{3} \mid Y_{1},Y_{2}) \\
&= P(Y_{2}) P(Y_{1} \mid Y_{2}) P(Y_{3} \mid Y_{1},Y_{2}) \\
&= \dots\\
&= P(Y_{3}) P(Y_{2} \mid Y_{3}) P(Y_{1} \mid Y_{2},Y_{3})
\end{align}$$

The order of the variables can be represented by directed graphs.

We got parent decomposition!
