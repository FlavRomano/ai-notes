When the target-space $\mathcal{T}$ is **continuous** e.g the real line $\mathbb{R}$
- it is more natural to specify the probability that a random variable $X$ is in an interval $$P(a \le X \le b) \quad a<b$$
- the expression $P(X\le x)$ is known as the **cumulative distribution function**
