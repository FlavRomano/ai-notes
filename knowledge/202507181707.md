Salting
- It's an advanced technique
- used to mitigate 
	- early wide-transformation like groupByKey that can results in unbalanced partitions
	- essentially, any key-skewed situation
- is used to spread across different nodes elements that are in the same partition.  

Unbalanced partitions deteriorate performance of operations in general and doesn't take advantage of narrow-transformation independency.
