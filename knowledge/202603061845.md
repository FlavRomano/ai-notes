As name suggests, maximum likelihood want's to maximize the likelihood term $P(d\mid X)$ 

> how well a hypothesis explains the observed data

We want to find the best hypothesis that makes the observed data most probable.

$$h_{ML} = \arg \max_{h\in H} P(d \mid h)$$

> Maximum Likelihood is a special case of MAP where the prior is uniform
> - we assume that ALL hypothesis are equally likely before seeing data $$\forall i,j\; P(h_{i})=P(h_{j})$$

Is the most prone inference method to overfitting, because ignores the priors. Is in fact completely data-driven.
