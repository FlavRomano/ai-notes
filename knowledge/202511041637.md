The primal form of the problem is the following:

> Given the training samples $T=\{(x_{i},d_{i}) \mid \forall i=1\dots N\}$ 
> - find optimum values of $w$ and $b$
> - which minimize the objective function$$\psi(w) = \frac{1}{2} w^T w \implies min\;\lvert \lvert w \rvert  \rvert $$
> 	- $\psi(w)$ is *quadratic* and *convex* in $w$
> - and satisfies the separating hyperplane constraints $$d_{i}(w^T x_{i} + b) \geq 1\quad i=1\dots N$$
> 	- constraints are linear in $w$

see. [[202511041714|dual form]] to compute Lagrangian multipliers for the solution.
