Gradient clipping is an heuristic to prevent catapult effect of gradient descent, when 
- gradient is massive
- update becomes massive
	- weights move far away into a different and worse region of space
	- this can causing loss to diverge to infinity

![[Pasted image 20260223181618.png]]

We define a threshold $v$ for the gradient length.

If the gradient becomes massive
- rescale the gradient to
	- preserve direction
	- limits the steps
		- no catapult effect anymore
