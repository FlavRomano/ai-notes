1. Initialize $k$ centers randomly
2. Assignment
	- assign every pattern in the dataset to its closet center
3. Update
	- recompute the center to be centroid (geometric mean) of all points currently assigned to it
4. GOTO 2 until convergence
	- centroids don't change anymore
5. If no convergence
	- good enough when minimal decrease in error
	- not improving anymore
