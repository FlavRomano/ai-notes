A causal bayesian network is a bayesian network where 
- each edge represents direct causality between variables $$Y_{1} \rightarrow Y_{2} \implies Y_{1}\;\text{directly causes}\;Y_{2}$$

![[Drawing 2026-03-09 21.35.26.excalidraw]]

The two models $G_1$ and $G_2$ denote 
- **equivalent** bayesian networks but
- **distinct** causal bayesian networks

In a causal bayesian network variables are **determined by their parents**
