The product-rule relates the joint distribution to the conditional distribution $$p(x,y) = P(y \mid x)P(x) = P(x \mid y)P(y)$$ so every joint distribution of two random variables can be written as product of two other distributions:
1. marginal distribution of the first random variable $$p(x)$$
2. conditional distribution of the second random variable given the first $$P(y \mid x)$$

The bayes rule is a direct consequence of this rule $$p(x,y) = P(x \mid y)P(y) = P(y \mid x) P(x)$$ $$\implies P(x \mid y) = \frac{P(y \mid x)P(x)}{P(y)}$$
