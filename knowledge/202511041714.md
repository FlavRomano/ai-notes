Given the training samples $T=\{(x_{i},d_{i}) \mid \forall i=1\dots N\}$ 
- find the optimum Lagrangian multipliers $$\{\alpha_{i}\} \quad \forall i=1\dots N$$
- which **maximize** $$max\; Q(\alpha)$$ with objective function equals to $$Q(\alpha) = \sum_{i=1} ^ N \alpha_{i} - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{j=1}^N \alpha_{i} \alpha_{j} d_{i} d_{j} x_{i}^T x_{j}$$
- satisfying the constraints
	- all positive Lagrangian multipliers $$\alpha_{i} \geq 0 \quad\forall i=1\dots N$$
	- $$\sum_{i=1}^N \alpha_{i} d_{i} =0$$ ensures that the **net effect** of the Lagrange multipliers over both classes **balances out**.
		- Keeps the hyperplane centered between support vectors of opposite classes
		- Prevents class bias
