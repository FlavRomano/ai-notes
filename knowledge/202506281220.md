Given two random variables $X:\Omega \to \mathcal{T}_{X}$, $Y:\Omega \to \mathcal{T}_{Y}$

- the **marginal probability** that $X$ takes the value $x$ **irrespective** of the value of random variable $Y$ is written $p(x)$
- we write $X \sim p(x)$ to denote that *the random variable $X$ is distributed according to $p(x)$*
