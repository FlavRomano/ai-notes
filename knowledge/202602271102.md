Hebbian learning part. We move the weights closer to $x$, at iteration $t$  $$w_{i}(t+1) = w_{i}(t) + \eta(t) \cdot h_{i,i^*}(t) \cdot(x-w_{i}(t))$$ 

![[Pasted image 20260227104802.png]]

where
- $h_{i,i^*}(t)$ is the **neighborhood function** (or kernel)
	- usually a gaussian centered on the winner $N(\mu=w_{i^*}(t), \sigma^2_{nh}(t))$
		- the radius $\sigma$ shrinks over time
		- start ordering the map globally
			- large neighbors
		- refine locally
			- small neighbors

![[Pasted image 20260227105857.png]]

Considering the unit $i$
- if it's the winner
	- $h=1$
	- **max update**
- if it's *next to* the winner
	- $h\to 1$
	- **strong update**
- if it's *far away on the grid*
	- $h\to 0$
	- **no update**
