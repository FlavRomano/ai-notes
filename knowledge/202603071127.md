> The categorical distribution generalises the Bernoulli distribution to more than two states. 

For a discrete variable $x$, with symbolic states $dom(x) = \{ 1, \dots, C \}$ $$p(x=c) = \theta_{c}, \quad \sum_{c} \theta_{c} = 1$$

The dirichlet is the conjugate to this distribution
