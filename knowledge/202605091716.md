We add a penalty term to the cost function $$J_{SAE}(\theta) = \sum_{x\in D} (L(x, \tilde{x}) + \underbrace{\lambda \Omega (z(x))}_{\text{penalty}})$$

to penalize $z$, typically $$\Omega(z(x)) = \Omega(f(x)) = \sum_{j} \lvert z_{j}(x) \rvert $$

Training with regularization is similar to MAP.
