> Each node is conditionally independent of all its non-descendants given a joint state of its parents. $$Y_{v} \perp Y_{V\setminus ch(v)}\mid Y_{pa(v)}, \;\forall v\in V$$

For a node $v\in V$
- if we know the state of its parents $Y_{pa(v)}$
	- the node $Y_{v}$ becomes independent of the rest of the network 
	- excluding its own children$$Y_{V \setminus ch(v)}$$
