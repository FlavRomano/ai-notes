The dirichlet is continuos distribution on probability distributions $$\alpha = (\alpha_{1},\dots,\alpha_{Q}),\quad \alpha_{i}\ge 0, \quad\sum_{i}\alpha_{i}=1$$

$$p(\alpha) = \frac{1}{Z(u)} \delta\left( \sum_{i=1}^Q \alpha_{i} - 1 \right) \prod_{q=1}^Q \alpha_{q}^{u_{q}-1} \mathbb{I}[\alpha_{q} \ge 0]$$ 

Where $$Z(u) = \frac{\prod_{q=1}^Q\Gamma(u_{q})}{\Gamma\left( \sum_{q=1}^Q u_{q} \right)}$$

Conventionally we denote the distribution as $$Dirichlet(\alpha\mid u)$$
- the parameter $u$ controls how strongly the mass of the distribution is pushed to the corners of the simplex
	- $u_{q} = 1$ for all $q$ corresponds to a uniform distribution

In the binary case $Q=2$, the distribution is equivalent to a Beta distribution.

- The product of two dirichlet is another dirichlet
- The marginal of a dirichlet is also a dirichlet
- The marginal of a single dirichlet component $\theta_{i}$ is a Beta distribution $$p(\theta_{i}) = B\left( \theta_{i}\mid u_{i}, \sum_{j\ne i} u_{j} \right)$$
