The univariate gaussian distribution is defined as $$p(x\mid \mu,\sigma^2) = N(x\mid\mu,\sigma^2) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\pi \sigma^2 }} \exp \left( -\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right)$$ where $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$ are mean and variance of the distribution. $$\mu=\mathbb{E}[x], \quad \sigma^2=\mathbb{E}[(x-\mu)^2]$$

For $\mu=0$ and $\sigma^2=1$, we got the standard normal distribution.
