The M-step is the last phase of expectation maximization, in this phase we update the model parameters based on the maximization of expected complete log likelihood estimation $Q$.

$$\theta^{(k+1)} = \arg \max_{\theta} Q(\theta \mid \theta^{(k)})$$

We are indirectly maximizing the complete log likelihood, because $Q$ is a proxy of $\mathcal{L}_{c}$. And at time $\theta^{(k)}$ both function touches.
