A slack variable is a 
- non-negative scalar $$\xi_{i} \geq 0\; \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
- represent the distance between
	- one point inside the margin
	- the closest border (either negative or positive) $$d_{i}(w^T x_{i} + b) \geq 1 - \xi_{i}\quad \forall i = 1 \dots N$$
