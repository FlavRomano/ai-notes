Opposite of symbolic representation. 

![[Pasted image 20260223175843.png]]

Concepts are represented by 
- a **pattern of activations** across many neurons
- aka dense vector

Which allows two main benefit:
1. Similarity
	- feature sharing between similar vectors
	- improve generalization
	- e.g cat and dog, blue car and red car
2. Exponential Capability
	- model can distinguish exponentially more regions in input space
	- with less samples
