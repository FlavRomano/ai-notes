Compare input $x$ to all weights $w$, find index $i^*$  on the map that minimizes the euclidean distance. Produce winner $w_{i^*(x)}$

That's a *winner take all strategy* $$i^*(x) = \arg\min_{i} \lvert \lvert x-w_{i} \rvert  \rvert $$
