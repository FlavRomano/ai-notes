The beta distribution is a continuous probability distribution defined on $[0,1]$ in terms of **two positive parameters** $\alpha, \beta$ that control the shape of the distribution. 

$$p(x\mid \alpha,\beta) = B(x\mid \alpha, \beta) = \frac{1}{B(\alpha,\beta)}x^{\alpha-1} (1-x)^{\beta - 1}, \quad 0\le x\le 1$$

Where the beta function is function is defined as $$B(\alpha,\beta) = \frac{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}$$

> The beta distribution is a suitable model for the random behavior of percentages and proportions

The generalization to multiple variables is called a Dirichlet distribution.

Is the conjugate prior probability distribution of Bernoulli and Binomial
