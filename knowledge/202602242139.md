This is the theorem that justify the grounds of randomized neural networks.

> A complex pattern-classification problem, cast in a high-dimensional space nonlinearly, is more likely to be linearly separable than in a low-dimensional space (provide that the space is not crowded)

This is the same property exploited by SVM Kernels.
