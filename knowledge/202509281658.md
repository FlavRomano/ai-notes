A learner **overfits** the data when it outputs a hypothesis $h(\cdot)\in H$ that
- generalize bad on unseen data
	- generalization error (risk) $R$ is high
- is accurate on training set
	- empirical (training) error $E$ is low
	- fits well the training data

But exists another hypothesis $h'(\cdot)\in H$ having
- empirical error higher than $h$ $$E'>E$$ it fits worse the training data
- generalization risk lower than $h$ $$R'<R$$

So $h'$ generalize better despite the worst fitting.
