The Bernoulli distribution concerns a discrete binary variable $x$ with $dom(x) = \{ 0,1 \}$ where $$p(x=1) = \theta, \quad mean(x) = \theta, \quad var(x) = \theta(1-\theta)$$

The Beta distribution is the conjugate prior.
