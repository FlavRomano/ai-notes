The inductive bias of K-nn is determined by 
1. **metric**
	- is a language bias
	- dictates which examples are considered the most similar to the unseen input
	- k-nn is fragile
		- with respect of basic preprocessing (like scaling the input space)
		- can have high impact and effectively changes the metric used ![[Pasted image 20251005170053.png]]
2. **locality**
	- this bias assumes a local approximation
	- it operates under the assumption that
		- a point's classification 
		- should be similar to the classification of its immediate neighbors
	- local estimations, no global hypothesis
