1. We must pick $K$ number of clusters in advance
2. Local minima, bad initialization means bad results
3. No visualization property
	- centroids have no relation to each other
	- e.g Cluster 1 *isn't next to* Cluster 2
4. Assumes spherical clusters
	- data can be shaped in ring or banana ![[Pasted image 20260227100354.png]]
	- k-means too simple
