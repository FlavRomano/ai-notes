A **cumulative distribution function** of a multivariate real-valued random variable $X$ with states $x\in \mathbb{R}^D$ is given by $$F_{X}(x) = P(X_{1} \le x_{1}, \dots, X_{D}\le x_{d})$$
where 
- $X=(X_{1}, \dots, X_{D})^T$
- $x=(x_{1}, \dots, x_{D})^T$
- the right-hand side represents the probability that a random variable $X_{i}$ **takes the value smaller than or equal to $x_{i}$**

The cdf can be expressed also as the **integral of the probability density function $f(x)$** $$ F_{X}(x) = \int_{-\infty}^{x_{1}} \dots \int_{\infty}^{x_{D}} f(z_{1},\dots,z_{D}) dz_{1}\dots dz_{D}$$
