Consider a multi-state variable $x$ with $dom(x) = \{ 1,\dots,K \}$ with corresponding state probabilities $\theta_{1}, \dots, \theta_{K}$.

![[Pasted image 20260307151532.png]]

We then draw $n$ samples from this distribution, the probability of observing
- the state $1$ $y_{1}$ times
- the state $2$ $y_{2}$ times
- ...
- the state $K$ $y_{K}$ times

 in the $n$ samples is $$p(y_{1}, \dots, y_{K}\mid \theta) = \frac{n!}{y_{1}!\dots y_{K}!} \prod_{i=1}^K  \theta^{y_{i}}_{i}$$

Where $$n = \sum_{i=1}^K y_{i}$$ the mean and variance are $$mean(y_{i}) = n \theta_{i}\quad var(y_{i}) = n \theta _{i}(1-\theta_{i})$$ 

The Dirichlet distribution is the conjugate prior for the multinomial distribution.
