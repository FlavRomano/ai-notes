It solves smoothing problem. Given a sequence we want the single state at time $t$. The posterior.

We could exploit the factorization

![[Drawing 2026-06-12 18.36.15.excalidraw]]

$$\begin{align}
P(S_{t} = i \mid \mathbf{y}) &= P(S_{t} = i, Y_{1:t}, Y_{t+1:T}) \\
&= P(S_{t} = i, Y_{1:t}) P(Y_{t+1:T} \mid S_{t} = i)  \\
&= \alpha_{t}(i) \beta_{t}(i)
\end{align}$$

- $\alpha_{t}(i)$ **forward message**
	- probability of arriving at state $i$ at time $t$ after observing all the past up to $t$
	- how likely is to arrive at $i$ starting from the beginning of the sequence
	- base case $$\alpha_{1}(i) = \pi_{i} b_{i}(y_{1})$$ in other words to start at state $i$ I should
		1. starting from $i$
		2. generating $y_1$
	- recursive case $$\alpha_{t}(i) = b_{i}(y_{t}) \sum_{j=1}^C A_{ij} \alpha_{t-1}(j)$$ in other words to arrive at state $i$ I should
		1. come from any other previous state
		2. generating the current observation at state $i$
- $\beta_{t}(i)$ **backward message**
	- if I'm at state $i$ at time $t$, how likely is to observe all the future?
	- how well the current state explains what will happen next.

So a state is plausible if explains well 
- the past
- the future

If one of them is improbable, then the posterior becomes little.
