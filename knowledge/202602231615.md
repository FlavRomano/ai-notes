K-nn and SVM rely on smoothness assumption (local constancy)
- if two points are near each other
- then they have the same label

But in high dimensional space, data points are sparse
- we need more samples to cover the space
	- exponential number of samples
	- $O(k)$ samples for $O(k)$ regions

Deep networks assume compositionality, they can generalize non locally
- with $O(k)$ samples they distinguish $O(2^k)$ regions
- they suffer less the curse of dimensionality
