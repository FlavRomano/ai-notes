We have a map, the weights are initialized randomly.

We draw an input $x$
1. competitive stage
	- find the winner 
	- unit most similar to $x$
2. cooperative stage
	- key difference from k-means
	- upgrade the weights
		- of the winner **AND** of the units that are neighbors on the map
