Tikhonov regularization is a mathematical framework that
- implements a control of model complexity
	- less VC-dimension
	- using just one hyper-parameter more
- introduce a penalty term that 
	- penalizes high value of the weights
	- tends to drive all the weights to smaller values

$$Loss(w) = \sum_{p=1}^l (y_{p} - x_{p}^T w)^2 + \lambda \lvert \lvert w \rvert  \rvert^2,\quad \lvert \lvert w \rvert  \rvert^2 = \sum w_{j}^2,\quad \lambda \geq 0 $$
The important thing is balancing $\lambda$
- small $\lambda$
	- penalty $\lambda \lvert \lvert w \rvert \rvert^2$ weak
	- focus on minimizing training error
	- $w$ become large and unstable
	- bias is low
		- model fits training data well
	- variance is high 
		- model does not generalize well
- large $\lambda$
	- penalty $\lambda \lvert \lvert w \rvert \rvert^2$ dominates
	- model **shrinks coefficients** toward zero
	- predictions become smoother
		- less sensitive to noise
	- if very large, model becomes **too rigid**
		- bias increase
			- underfitting
