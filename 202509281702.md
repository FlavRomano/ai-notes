In supervised learning, 
- we aim to approximate an unknown target function $f$ 
- that maps input patterns $x$ to desired outputs $d$. 

---

Our model $h_w(x)$
- parameterized by $w$
- should be as close as possible to $f(x)$.

We want to measure 
- distance between $h_{w}(x)$ and $d$
- is quantified by a [[202509281707|loss function]]
