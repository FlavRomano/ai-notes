1. Start with 
	- weight vector $w_{initial}$ small 
	- step size $\eta\in(0,1)$
2. Compute $$\Delta w = - \nabla E(w) = - \frac{\partial E(w)}{\partial w}$$
3. Compute $$w_{new} = w + (\eta \cdot \Delta w)$$
4. GOTO 2 until convergence or $E(w)$ is sufficiently small
