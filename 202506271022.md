The event space $\mathcal{A}$ 
- is the space of potential results of the experiment
- a subset of sample space $A\subset \Omega$ is in the event space $\mathcal{A}$ 
	- if at the end of the experiment we can observe whether a particular outcome $\omega \in \Omega$ is in $A$
- $\mathcal{A}$ is obtained by considering the collection of subsets of $\Omega$
- in discrete probability distributions $\mathcal{A}$ is often the power set of $\Omega$
