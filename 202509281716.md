- Since training involves multiple samples, we compute the **average loss** across the dataset.
- For a training set of $l$ samples: $$Loss(h_w​)=E(w)= \frac{1}{l}  \sum_{p=1}^l L(h_{w​}(x_{p}​),d_{p}​)$$ This is also called the **empirical risk** (or empirical error).

---

e.g: Given $<x1​,d1​>$ and $<x_2, d_2>$, the empirical loss is:

$$\frac{L(h_{w}(x_{1}), d_{1}) + L(h_{w}(x_{2}), d_{2})}{2}$$
