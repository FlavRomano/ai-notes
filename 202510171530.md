The SGD, as any iterative approach, can is generally efficient but depends on 
- the n. of training data
- the n. of epochs

there are a lot of other heuristics approaches but...

It's still better of other methods for the saddle points
- saddle points can grow exponentially with the number of dimension
- it's an issue for a lot of heuristics approaches
	- e.g newton's method, if it falls on it everything stops.

The SGD escapes the saddles points, falling in local minima part.

The go to approach, at least as a starting point, is the SGD with momentum (with regularization). As a comparison toward other approaches.
